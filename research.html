MS in Medicine — Thesis Research

Department of Medicine, Bangladesh Agricultural University · Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Taohidul Islam

Prevalence and antimicrobial resistance pattern of Acinetobacter baumannii from veterinary hospitals and pet animals in Bangladesh.

  • Detected A. baumannii from rectal and oral samples of dogs and cats, plus environmental and waste samples from veterinary hospitals
  • Characterised phenotypic and genotypic resistance patterns and traced epidemiologic factors contributing to pathogen persistence
  • Wrote and independently secured the National Science and Technology Fellowship (2025) to fund the project
